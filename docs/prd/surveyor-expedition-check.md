## Demonstrated socket lifetime correction

The first valid connected expedition reaches real far save, but Snapshot reports
an error while reading a joined chassis's original frame sockets. Joining a
standalone piece indexes its custom mount socket in `_own`. Later ordinary
removal queues that socket for deletion without removing the ownership entry.
A minimal two-piece mounted-beacon reproduction fails in the same way after two
physics ticks, independently of arrival, Home and weather.

Correct ownership at the removal boundary: Chassis removes the exact custom
socket from its original piece's `_own` index before Device removes/frees it.
Frame-socket iteration also checks current validity before type inspection.
Preserve socket order, joins, fitted contents and save serialization semantics;
do not filter the save or suppress script errors. Verify the small removal/save
regression and the full original-pod Home expedition afterward.
