## FTL ruling

Two regimes, replacing "no FTL" in `lore-research.md`:

- **Information** crosses near-instantly via quantum channels.
- **Anything with mass** crosses at one fixed slow rate regardless of size. Lane crossings take years.

Consequences: bigger hulls are strictly more economical, so freighter towns are the natural design. Frontier AI could run live over StarCom; when the relay died, only local models remained. This resolves the open "no FTL against StarCom" question.

## Factions

**United Government.** A federation of interstellar human governments: a mixed bag of well-meaning, jaded, opportunistic and exploration-minded people. It begins as a loose federation and hardens into a united front during the war; early ruins show mismatched member-state and corporate markings, war-era materiel is standardised and severe. Slow and bureaucratic. Runs the federal customs port facing the hive mind.

**The Provisional State.** The UG frontier province containing XN-118. Formed at unification as an emergency administration to absorb the border territory of a pre-unification state; the "provisional" label stuck for centuries. Not a direct border system (no customs on Zan), but it sits on an important He-3 lane. Harsher and somewhat authoritarian; hungry for power and resources to project onto its claims, which the war partly vindicates. It monitors the lanes through its systems.

**The governor-general.** Ex-military logistics officer, appointed to hold the frontier, now more powerful than the post intended. A pragmatist, not a villain: sees the hive mind as a real threat and the UG as asleep. Authority rests on a never-revoked emergency mandate. Knew about Port Morgan and let it be.

**TILG.** The rising giant shipping line (Hapag-Lloyd analogue). Thrives in peacetime with bigger, modern ships. Registers Zan only as a *temporary transshipment hub*, citing the unstable orbit to avoid the Provisional's military upkeep (troop stations, customs, a de facto second tax). The excuse turns out to be true.

**Standard Freight.** A former giant line from before unification, tied to the vanished state the Provisional replaced. Older, more militarised fleet with compliant, federally aligned ships. Has been losing to TILG for about a century because it won't adapt. Competes with TILG on the lane but also takes subcontracted dirty, risky runs. The governor has ties to it. The war gives it a brief, bitter comeback. Ruins: heavy, overbuilt, plainly stamped hulls.

**AI hive mind.** Exported He-3 to humans (it doesn't need it as they do) and imported human goods to study humanity in order to conquer it. It started the war.

## History of Zan

1. **Colonist era.** UG governments and corporations survey Zan, know about the changing orbit and volcanism, and build for eruptions. Port Morgan grows unregistered beyond the approved hub. Best loot.
2. **War.** The hive mind attacks; the lane dies. A federal order evacuates the port station. Unregistered residents can dodge the draft, so Zan becomes a relative refuge and a hive of scum and villainy.
3. **Tithe and mercenary era.** The Provisional tolerates Zan by squeezing it: a war tithe (He-3, refinery output), and Zan works with the Provisional as mercenaries and as an off-the-books back door. The Provisional runs a black-site prison on Zan. Tithe visits and draft-dodgers cause internal skirmishes.
4. **Withdrawal.** After a major war defeat the Provisional retreats and can no longer support Zan, though the enemy never invades.
5. **Collapse.** Infrastructure designed for eruptions only works with a supply chain behind it. Ordinary eruptions, the kind it was built to survive, now hit with no maintenance or imports. The overgrown population collapses within a generation or two. The paroxysm stays canon; abandonment is the real killer.
6. **Bronze Age eras.** Scattered survivors rebuild through two Bronze Age-like feudal eras (Kenshi-like), dying out over 10–15k years to the changing environment.

Military tech on Zan is extremely rare and arrives only via deserters (and possibly lost tithe collectors). The war itself stays offstage; it reaches Zan only through who shows up.

## Surface today

- **Burial, not erosion.** Sand seas fill valleys and blanket old volcanic terrain; the surface looks flat while rough ground sits beneath.
- **Preservation.** Dry, low-oxygen sand preserves ruins; corporate tech uses tough alloys and sealed casings. Migrating dunes and storms expose and rebury sites, which is the harsher-weather-uncovers-deeper-loot mechanic.
- **Loot strata.** Corporate tech deepest and rarest, skimmer-era salvage in the middle, Bronze Age forged goods near the surface.
- **The clock.** The outgassed atmosphere is slowly leaking to space; storms last only while air remains. The player arrives tens of thousands of years after the last survivors, with the window closing. Why the player is there is deliberately unknown.
